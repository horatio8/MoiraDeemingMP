The Case Against VNI West

This isn't a transmission line. It's a top-down imposition on the West.

The Victoria–NSW Interconnect West (VNI West) is being pushed through with the kind of speed Governments reserve for things they don't want scrutinised. The route bisects irreplaceable farmland. The poles and wires are larger than anything previously built in the region. And the consultation has been a tick-box exercise on a foregone conclusion.

The people whose land is being taken should not be the last people consulted. We will not accept a process that treats Western Victoria as an empty corridor. — Moira Deeming MP, Legislative Council

What I'm asking the Parliament to do

  • 1Halt VNI West works pending a genuinely independent route assessment, including underground options.
  • 2Mandate full disclosure of all route-selection cost-benefit modelling and environmental modelling.
  • 3Establish a statutory community consultation right — binding, not advisory — before any further land access notices.
  • 4Compensation parity with mining and pipeline easements, indexed for the life of the asset.
  • 5Independent review of the cumulative impact on farming communities across the corridor.

Why your signature matters

Your signature is not symbolic. It is filed publicly with my office and tabled in the Legislative Council under Petition Standing Orders. Each name is recorded against the petition that the Government has so far refused to debate. The bigger the petition, the harder it is to keep ignoring.

I represent Western Metropolitan Region in the Victorian Legislative Council. My job is to take this fight directly to the floor of the Parliament — and I cannot do it without you.
