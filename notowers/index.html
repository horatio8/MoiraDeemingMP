The Campaign

This isn't a transmission line. It's an imposition on the West.

The Victoria–NSW Interconnect West (VNI West) is being pushed through with the kind of speed Governments reserve for things they don't want scrutinised. The route bisects irreplaceable farmland. The poles and wires are larger than anything previously built in the region. And the consultation has been a tick-box exercise on a foregone conclusion.

I'm calling on the Government to stop this project immediately, table the underlying modelling, and put the underground alternative on the same level of disclosure as the overhead route before another acquisition notice is served on a Victorian farming family.

The people whose land is being taken should not be the last people consulted. We will not accept a process that treats Western Victoria as an empty corridor. — Moira Deeming MP, Legislative Council

What I'm asking the Parliament to do

  • 1Immediately halt all VNI West works, surveys and acquisition notices pending an independent route review.
  • 2Disclose, in full, every route-selection cost-benefit and environmental model relied on to date.
  • 3Cost the underground alternative on the same basis as the overhead route, and publish the comparison.
  • 4Establish a binding statutory community consultation right before any further land access.
  • 5Compensation parity with mining and pipeline easements, indexed for the life of the asset.
  • 6Independent assessment of the cumulative impact on farming communities across the entire corridor.

Why your signature matters

A petition tabled in the Legislative Council is filed publicly with my office and recorded in the Council's papers. Every name signed against this project is a name the Government has to walk past on its way into the chamber. The bigger the petition, the harder it becomes to keep ignoring.

I represent Western Metropolitan Region in the Victorian Legislative Council. My job is to take this fight directly to the floor of the Parliament — and I cannot do it without you.
