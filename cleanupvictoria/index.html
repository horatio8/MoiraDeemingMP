The Campaign

Western Melbourne deserves better than catch-and-release.

Home invasions, carjackings, and youth crime are tearing through suburbs that used to be safe. The Government promises reviews. The Opposition promises statements. I am taking the fight directly to the Parliament — and I need your name on the petition to do it.

Your signature is filed publicly with my office and tabled in the Legislative Council under the Council's Petition Standing Orders. It is the most direct way constituents have to put pressure on the Premier to act.

The first job of a State Government is the safety of its own people. Right now, on every measure that matters, Victoria is failing the people of the West. — Moira Deeming MP, Legislative Council

What I'm asking the Parliament to do

  • 1Mandatory minimum sentences for repeat violent offences and weapons offences.
  • 2End the bail-and-release loop for serious recidivists — restore the presumption against bail for repeat indictable offending.
  • 3Fund 500 additional frontline officers for the Western suburbs, with deployment audited annually.
  • 4Statutory right of victims to be notified and heard at every bail and sentencing decision.
  • 5Independent review of every juvenile bail decision granted in 2024–25, with findings tabled.

Why your signature matters

A petition tabled in the Legislative Council with thousands of names is harder to ignore than a press release. Every name is recorded against the petition the Government has so far refused to debate. The bigger the petition, the more difficult it becomes for the Premier to keep walking past it.
